WIP

In the past year, I have been delivering a few hands on demos on Jdaviz to the astronomical community. I targeted mainly institutions with graduate students and postdocs
who might not know about new tools and could benefit from some analysis and visualization software that is modern and well maintained...like Jdaviz.
At the last demo, I decided to finally put the material in the open! Said material can be found at the 
[jdaviz_demo github repository](https://github.com/spacetelescope/jdaviz_demo/tree/main) and includes installation instructions and code to download
the necessary data. Big thanks to Jesse Averbukh, Hatice Karatay, and Clare Shanahan who put together the Jdaviz notebook in the repo!

<small>The image shows the cube of NGC7469, a galaxy with an active galactic nucleus in the center, 
observed as part of [program 1328](https://www.stsci.edu/jwst/science-execution/approved-programs/dd-ers/program-1328). 
The top left viewer shows the flux cube at 2.13 micron and the three spatial subsets used for the analysis. 
The top right viewer shows the calculated velocity map of the Paschen-alpha line obtained with the Moment Map plugin. 
The bottom viewer shows the spectra extracted in the center (red spectrum) and in the outskirt 
clump (blue spectrum) using the newly updated Spectral Extraction plugin. To the right, the 
plugin tray is open, showing all the visualization and analysis plugins available in the tool.</small>
